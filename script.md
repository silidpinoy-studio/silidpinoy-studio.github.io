# Reel 1 — "This is for the ones who stay late"

**Length:** about 2–2.5 minutes spoken slowly  
**Tone:** quiet, honest, Filipino-teacher-real, not melodramatic  
**Purpose:** Teachers' Day gift video + follower growth for the Facebook Page

---

It's probably still dark outside when you get home.

And even then, the day isn't really over.

There's a test to finish.  
A lesson plan that's due.  
A TOS to build from scratch.  
Another activity sheet.  
Another PowerPoint.  
Another thing that has to look presentable before tomorrow.

If you're a teacher, you know that feeling.  
The one where your real work starts after everyone else has gone home.

I made this because I kept seeing that.  
Not from far away. Close enough to know how heavy it gets.

The parts of teaching nobody posts about.  
The paperwork.  
The formatting.  
The same documents, rebuilt again and again, every quarter, every grading period.

You don't just teach.  
You build the materials that let you teach.  
And somewhere along the way, that became a second job nobody applauds.

So for Teachers' Day, I didn't want to give you a card.  
I wanted to give you something that actually takes a little of that weight off.

This is SilidPinoy.  
It's a set of tools for teachers — built to handle the things that steal your evenings.

And today, it's a gift.  
Free.  
For the teachers who've been carrying too much for too long.

Here's what's inside.

First — **Test Creator**.  
You give it a topic, a grade level, a subject, or even upload your module, and it helps you build a real summative or formative test.  
Multiple choice, true or false, matching, identification, essay — the kinds of items you actually use.  
It doesn't pretend to replace your judgment. It helps you get to the finished test faster.

Second — **TOS Maker**.  
If you've ever spent an evening trying to build a Table of Specifications that actually lines up with your competencies and Bloom's levels, this one is for you.  
It helps you build that alignment instead of wrestling with it for hours.

Third — **iLaw Lesson Plan**.  
DepEd-aligned lesson plans, drafted in minutes, not hours.  
Not so you stop doing your job — so you stop starting every lesson plan from zero at 9 p.m.

Fourth — **PPT Maker**.  
Turn a topic, a DLL, an exam, even a quiz into a clean slide deck.  
The one you'd normally be rebuilding after dinner.

And fifth — **Activity Sheet Maker**.  
Take a lesson and turn it into a module-style worksheet, with puzzles, tables, the kinds of visuals that make a paper feel finished instead of pasted together.

Those are the tools.  
They live in one place, and they're meant for the real rhythm of a teacher's week — not a fantasy version of it.

I'm giving this away because I meant it when I said I wanted to take some of the weight off.

But I also want to be honest with you.  
This free set is the gift.  
There's a fuller version coming for teachers who want more — one subscription, more tools, the heavier lifting handled in one place.

I'm not asking you to buy anything today.  
I'm telling you it's there, because teachers deserve to know what's possible before anyone ever asks them to pay.

If this helped you even a little, I'd ask for two things.

One — **follow this page**.  
Not for a hard sell.  
Because the deeper tutorial is coming next, and I don't want you to miss it.  
I'm going to walk you through the tools one by one, the way you'd actually use them in a real school week.

Two — **like and share this with a teacher friend**.  
Not because I need the reach.  
Because there are teachers right now sitting at the same table you sat at tonight, building the same documents for the same reason.  
Maybe this can save one of them an evening.  
Maybe it can save a few.

Happy Teachers' Day.  
To the ones who stay late, redo the work, and still show up for the kids the next morning —  
this one's for you.

## Production notes

**Suggested video structure**
- 0:00–0:12 — Soft open. Quiet B-roll of a late-night desk if you have it.
- 0:12–0:35 — Sentiment and motive.
- 0:35–0:50 — Gift reveal.
- 0:50–1:45 — Walkthrough of the five tools.
- 1:45–2:05 — Honest teaser for the fuller/paid version.
- 2:05–end — Follow + like/share CTA, then the Teachers' Day closer.

**Visual options**
- You on camera, talking honestly, with occasional screen recordings.
- Or mostly screen + voiceover with a few human moments.
- Keep the first 12 seconds simple and real. Avoid a glam opening; this audience responds to being seen.

**Open items to finalize before recording**
1. Confirm the paid-tier timeline wording: "coming" vs "already live."
2. Match DepEd phrasing to your actual audience if it differs from "DepEd-aligned / TOS / DLL."
3. Decide whether the tutorial CTA points to a follow-up video series, a link, or both.
4. Pick the main follow destination — Facebook Page is the working default from our plan.
